03 — Literature

Literature

Mostly mystery and literary fiction.

I prefer works with an everyday texture over pure classical puzzles.

Bad endings are a particular delicacy.

Favorite Authors

  • Honobu Yonezawa

    米澤穂信

    My favorite author. Reading “Sayonara Yosei” in high school knocked me sideways

  • Kanae Minato

    湊かなえ

    The queen of bad endings, though I love her warmer works too. My pick, deliberately, is “Ofuku Shokan”

  • Risa Wataya

    綿矢りさ

    She is known for “The Back I Want to Kick”, but I am far too fond of “Yume o Ataeru”

  • Yo Ashizawa

    芦沢央

    My current top recommendation — she feels like the rightful heir to Yonezawa and Minato
