04 — What I Value

What I Value

  • 01

    A team moving toward a single shared goal

    • Especially teams where each member plays to their own character and fully owns their role
    • I dislike teams held together by cliquishness, inertia, or surface-level politeness
  • 02

    Building products

    • Above all, asking what makes a product good — and watching real users use something I helped build
  • 03

    Consistency

    • Or rather, maybe it is inconsistency that I dislike
  • 04

    Playfulness

    • My motto: have fun at work, and be serious about play
  • 05

    Competing, and caring about winning

    • Less about the result than the beauty of giving everything to win — and of people who do that colliding with each other
  • 06

    Unlike personalities reacting into something good

    • This, to me, is where the appeal of teamwork lives
  • 07

    Connecting things that look unrelated

    • I believe the essential fun of statistical physics lies exactly there
  • 08

    Things that are human and organic

    • I tend to fall for the creator more than the work itself
05 — Strengths

Strengths

  • Thinking, and seeing things from many angles

    • Particularly abstracting and generalizing phenomena, reasoning from data, and constructing plausible logic for decisions

  • Reading people

    • I can trace someone’s thinking and traits from how they speak and act, and work out what position suits them

  • Earning trust

    • I never rank one set of values above another and I treat everyone flatly, which tends to earn trust

  • Team building

    • When I lead, I aim for an environment where each member can proactively do what they want to do
    • I do not push guidance onto passive people — I tend to let them be
    • Less a manager than a producer, if I had to pick a word
